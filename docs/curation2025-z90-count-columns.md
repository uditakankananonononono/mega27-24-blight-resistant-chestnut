# Three columns, one ENA sample accession, 2026-09-28

In the [distributed chestnut expression archive](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/49310509), the paired-end raw-count matrix has separate columns for `SRR21681158`, `SRR21681184` and `SRR21681185`. [ENA metadata](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/portal/api/filereport?accession=PRJNA883560&result=read_run&fields=run_accession%2Csample_accession%2Csample_alias%2Csample_title%2Clibrary_strategy&format=tsv) links all three to one sample accession `SAMN30971621` (alias `Z90`). The matrix-only `SRR21681158` therefore has a real, nonidentical published count column among 33,991 gene rows, but is **not a new unique ENA sample accession**. We extracted just these three columns into a SHA-256-pinned, gzip-compressed table and replayed nonnegative integer counts, library-column sums and pairwise log(1+count) correlations. The archived whole Figshare ZIP matched its published MD5 at the earlier download, and the new extraction records that ZIP's SHA-256.

These are three run-labeled count columns from one ENA sample record, not three independent biological specimens. Their precise experimental relation beyond the shared accession is not established; the arithmetic is descriptive QC, not differential expression, blight resistance, an independent validation cohort or a comparator win. Underlying FASTQ payloads were not fetched, and there is no C24 gate credit.
