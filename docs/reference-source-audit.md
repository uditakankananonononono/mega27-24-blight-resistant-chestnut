# Chestnut primary-source identity screen, 2026-09-26

The [2023 Chinese chestnut early-infection article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9901152/) cites [PRJNA527178](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/portal/api/filereport?accession=PRJNA527178&result=read_run&fields=run_accession%2Cexperiment_accession%2Csample_accession%2Cstudy_accession%2Clibrary_strategy%2Cscientific_name&format=tsv) as the **reference genome for read alignment**, not as an accession list for its infection-assay samples. Live ENA metadata has 272 runs, all Castanea mollissima: 250 WGS, 20 RNA-Seq, 2 Hi-C. They are mixed-purpose reference-study metadata, not 272 independent infection datasets. Raw reads and the study's actual infection-assay data were not fetched here. The separate [2025 curated chestnut-genome paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12103606/) also cites the study, but a citation is not validation for a proposed resistance endpoint. Article XML, ENA report, hash audit and test are stored in this repo.

The relevant owner's [Darling 58 discontinuation notice](https://tacf.org/tacf-discontinues-development-of-darling-58/) and [performance page](https://tacf.org/darling-58-performance/) must be read before any historical Darling 58 success claim. This is an environmental-tree published-data analysis only, not a design or deployment plan. Next: locate original infection-assay accessions, compare study provenance and secure an independent validation cohort. No benchmark, discovery, or gate completion from this source screen.

## Original infection-project lead (2026-09-26)

On re-reading the original article's data statement, its *own transcriptomic sequencing data* are submitted to CNCB-NGDC under [PRJCA009200](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/bioproject/browse/PRJCA009200). The live project page links one GSA entry CRA006690, labeled "RNA-Seq of Chinese chestnut." This differs from PRJNA527178, the reference genome. The article describes 15 cDNA libraries across three time/condition groups, but neither run accessions nor payloads were recovered: the [GSA browse route](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/gsa/browse/CRA006690) returned SOURCE_NOT_AVAILABLE in the available fetch and a small client-side shell from direct HTTP. Archive and hash of the project page, original article, deterministic link check and replay test are included. A project/study identifier alone is not 15 independently verified records or evidence of infection outcomes. Next: source-access route or accessible mirrors, then assay-to-library mapping, data quality and prespecified independent comparison.

## GSA public search recovers experiment metadata, not reads (2026-09-27)

A source-grounded alternative to the client-side browse page is CNCB's [GSA search](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/gsa/search?searchTerm=CRA006690). Its experiment result page at [search/getSearchByAccession](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/gsa/search/getSearchByAccession?searchTerm=CRA006690) returns 15 CRX experiment IDs with source labels. The original article specifies five 0h / scratch-control 3h and 9h / inoculated 3h and 9h conditions, three libraries each; GSA labels split exactly 3 each under `Cm0`, `CmC3`, `CmC9`, `CmT3`, `CmT9`. The mnemonic-to-condition interpretation is plausible but still requires original study metadata confirmation. These pages are archived and SHA-256 checked; a replay script/test validates 15 unique CRX accessions and the balanced labels. The experiment labels are not raw read payloads, not independent studies, and not a biology result. Individual experiment detail routes still return only a small client shell through HTTP, so run accession/file retrieval remains open. No gate credit assigned.

## Older independent-cohort lead, sample identity conflict (2026-09-27)

[Barakat et al. 2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2688492/) compared infected canker with healthy stem in American and Chinese chestnut. [ENA per-experiment run reports](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/portal/api/filereport?accession=SRX001799&result=read_run&fields=run_accession%2Cexperiment_accession%2Csample_accession%2Cstudy_accession%2Clibrary_strategy%2Cscientific_name%2Cfastq_ftp%2Cfastq_bytes&format=tsv) list 14 runs across ten SRX001799–1808 experiment IDs; six are study experiments, while four within that contiguous range are not study experiments. The 2009 paper's canker paragraph gives accessions in order **American then Chinese** as SRX001804 and SRX001799, yet ENA records those two species as **Chinese then American**, respectively. The accession-to-condition mapping therefore cannot be trusted without original submission metadata or correction. The paper also uses 454 EST from canker tissue rather than 2023's 15-library early-infection RNA-seq, so it is at best an independent stress-test, not a comparable replicate. Article XML, live ENA report and checksum/test are archived; no reads were downloaded and gate credit remains zero. Preserve this mismatch, do not silently flip labels.

A follow-up [ENA original experiment report](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/portal/api/filereport?accession=SRX001804&result=read_experiment&fields=experiment_accession%2Crun_accession%2Csample_accession%2Cexperiment_title%2Csample_title%2Cscientific_name%2Cstudy_accession&format=tsv) strengthens the conflict: SRX001804's deposited title explicitly says Chinese chestnut canker and SRX001799's says American chestnut transcript fragment. The two original study experiment titles agree with ENA species labels, not with the species-order reading of the paper's canker paragraph. Even so, original deposition does not erase the article error. The report is archived, hashed, and checked in replay.

## Third-party file-index route for infection cohort, still no payload (2026-09-27)

The primary [GSA search experiment view](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/gsa/search/getSearchByAccession?searchTerm=CRA006690) supplies 15 CRX IDs and original source labels. A [third-party SeqOut TSV index](https://seqout.org/api/project/CRA006690/runs/download) maps all 15 primary CRX IDs one-to-one to 15 CRR runs, each with two HTTPS FASTQ file URLs: 30 advertised compressed files totaling **74,578,217,301 bytes**. A direct HTTP HEAD against the first primary download host file returned 200 with the exact byte count listed by the index (1,839,719,204 bytes). The other 29 URLs and MD5 hashes have **not** been checked against original GSA records or downloaded payloads. GSA's public browse route still yields an unavailable/security-check response for this session, so the run mapping remains a secondary-index lead, not verified primary accession payload. Archived TSV, hash, deterministic one-to-one join script and hermetic replay test are stored in this repo. It does not add fetched-and-used accession credit, expression results, independent validation or a resistance claim. This route is a feasibility lead for later authorized read-level analysis, not a cohort analysis itself.
