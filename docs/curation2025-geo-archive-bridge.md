# Four-series GEO-title to Figshare-column bridge, 2026-09-28

The [2025 chestnut data paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12103606/) cites GEO series GSE284510, GSE284516, GSE284517, and GSE284518. Archived NCBI GEO esummaries list 7, 18, 6 and 1 samples, respectively. Every one of their 32 sample titles begins with a distinct SRR run label; every one of those 32 labels appears among the 213 expression-column identifiers in the [distributed Figshare archive](https://springernature.figshare.com/articles/dataset/Comprehensive_curation_and_validation_of_genomic_datasets_for_chestnut/27060067). The remaining 181 Figshare column labels are not named by these four series' 32 GEO sample titles. This is a **metadata title-prefix bridge**, not proof that those 181 columns lack SRA records or source studies. The code records the 32 title-prefix comparisons; it does not fetch 213 individual accession payloads or inspect expression values, phenotypes or person-level independence. No comparator, discovery or gate credit.
